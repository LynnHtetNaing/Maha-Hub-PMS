About the Group

Built for hospitality, from every angle.

Maha Hub Group of Companies Limited was founded to serve the hospitality industry with the tools hotels depend on and the training their people grow through.

Exceptional hospitality is the result of two things working in harmony: well-connected systems and well-prepared people. Our two brands exist to support both, and we remain candid about what is available today and what is still being built.

Read about the Group

Our vision

A more connected, more capable hospitality industry.

Vision

To contribute to a more connected, innovative and professionally developed hospitality industry, beginning in Myanmar and expanding beyond.

Mission

To build practical technology and education that help hospitality businesses and their people operate with confidence, deliver exceptional service and grow.
