About Maha Hub

A hospitality technology platform for hotels and accommodation businesses.

What we do

Maha Hub is a hospitality technology platform focused on helping hotels and accommodation businesses manage their operations through connected digital systems.

Our goal is to simplify hotel technology by connecting operational, commercial and management functions within one ecosystem. Maha PMS sits at the core, and each Maha product is designed to work with the same hotel data instead of standing alone.

Customers subscribe to or purchase access to Maha software and related digital hospitality services. Today that includes Maha PMS, Maha Connect, Maha Booking and Maha Reach, with more products coming soon. Each product is opened for a hotel by Padauk.

Maha Hub is offered by Padauk Hospitality Technology and Academy. It is being designed for English, Thai and Myanmar.

More than software — a partner in your growth.

  • Clear about what is readyWe label every product as available now or coming soon, so customers can plan with confidence.
