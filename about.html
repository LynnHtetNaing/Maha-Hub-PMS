About the Group

A group for hospitality, in technology and education.

Maha Hub Group of Companies Limited brings software and learning together for hotels and the people who work in them.

Who we are

One group, two brands.

Maha Hub Group of Companies Limited is the parent of Maha Solutions and Maha Academy.

Maha Solutions is our technology brand. It offers connected software for hotels and accommodation businesses, starting with Maha PMS and growing into a family of products that share the same hotel data.

Maha Academy is our education brand. It is being built to offer practical hospitality education, from front-line skills to hotel management.

The pagoda in our mark stands for the heritage of the region we come from. The star above it stands for what we are building towards.
