About the Group

A group devoted to hospitality, in technology and in education.

Maha Hub Group of Companies Limited brings software and learning together, in service of hotels and the people who make them exceptional.

Who we are

One group, two brands.

Maha Hub Group of Companies Limited is the parent of Maha Solutions and Maha Academy.

Maha Solutions is our technology brand. It offers connected software for hotels and accommodation businesses, beginning with Maha PMS and growing into a family of products that share a single source of hotel data.

Maha Academy is our education brand. It is being created to offer practical hospitality education, from front-line skills to hotel management.

The convictions we build on.
