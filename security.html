Data security

How Maha Hub protects hotel and guest data: our principles, safeguards, providers and commitments.

Summary

Our commitment. Maha Hub is built in layers, so that each hotel's data is reachable only by the people that hotel authorises, travels over encrypted connections, and is handled by a small set of named infrastructure providers. If one layer is passed, the next one is still there.

  • Every person signs in with their own account. A new device is checked with a code sent to the user's email.
  • Maha Hub staff use an Authenticator app on top of their password.
  • Each hotel's data is separated in the database itself, not only on the screen.
  • Every Maha product is opened for a hotel individually by Maha Hub. Nothing is switched on by default.
  • Card payments are handled by Stripe. Maha Hub never receives or stores full card numbers.

Our security in layers

These are the safeguards we have put in place. Each one is described in plain words below.

1. Connection and hosting

  • Encrypted connections only. Maha Hub and its products are served only over HTTPS, and browsers are told to always use it.
  • Browser protections. Our pages send security headers that stop them being shown inside other websites, limit which scripts and services they may load, and keep the address of the page you came from private.
  • Network protection. Our websites and applications are delivered through Cloudflare, which adds content delivery and network protection.

2. Who can sign in

  • Individual accounts. Every user signs in with their own username and password. Shared logins should not be used.
  • Strong passwords. A password needs at least six characters, with a capital letter, a small letter, a special character and two numbers.
  • New device check. When a hotel user signs in from a new device, or after 30 days, a one-time code is sent to the user's email address and must be typed in before the system opens.
  • Safe password links. A link to set or reset a password works once and expires after a short time.

3. Extra checks for Maha staff

  • Authenticator app. People who administer Maha Hub must also enter a six-digit code from an Authenticator app. The same Authenticator works across the Maha systems.
  • Trusted devices. The Maha PMS and CRM ask for the code once on each browser and then trust that device for 14 days. The Maha Console, which controls the platform, asks for the code every time.
  • Control of lost devices. A trusted device can be forgotten, and an Authenticator can be reset by the platform owner.

4. Hotel separation

  • Separated in the database. Each hotel's records are protected by row-level rules in the database. The database itself decides who may read or change a record, so a screen or a button cannot open it by mistake.
  • Checked on the server. Access is verified again on our servers every time, not only in the browser.
  • Owner protected. The owner of a hotel account cannot be changed from the browser.
  • No card details in hotel data. The database refuses hotel data that contains card numbers, security codes or secret keys.
  • Tested. These rules are covered by automated tests that check that one hotel cannot see or change another hotel's data.

5. Roles, approvals and records

  • Role-based permissions. Roles such as manager, front desk and housekeeping limit what each user can see and change.
  • Shift-based sign-in. Front desk users sign in to a named shift, linking transactions and cash handling to the right person and time.
  • Approval for sensitive changes. Actions such as a refund, rebate, adjustment, voided payment or transfer can be sent for approval by a manager before they take effect.
  • Activity record. Important actions are recorded with who did them and when.
  • Prompt removal. Customers can remove users at any time, and should do so as soon as a staff member leaves.

6. Products opened per hotel

  • Nothing on by default. Maha PMS, Connect, Booking, Reach and Sales are opened for each hotel by Maha Hub, and each person is given only the products they need.
  • Held on the server. A product that is not opened for a hotel is closed on our servers, not only hidden on the screen.

7. Mail and booking links

  • Signed answer links. The buttons in a Maha Hub email, such as choosing a time, are signed for each recipient and cannot be used for anyone else.
  • Confirmation step. An answer is recorded only after the person confirms it on the page, so a mail scanner that opens a link does not answer for the hotel.
  • Unsubscribe. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and a hotel that unsubscribed is not sent to again.
  • Booking calendar. The Book a Demo and Talk with the Team pages hold no secret. They ask our servers for free times, and the servers check every request again.

Payments

Subscription payments and any card payments made through Maha are processed by Stripe. Maha Hub does not receive, store or have access to full payment card numbers or security codes. Older card-entry functions that stored card details have been switched off. Staff must never enter card numbers into notes, messages or free-text fields in Maha.

Providers

Maha Hub runs on established cloud infrastructure and uses a small number of specialist providers, each bound by its own security and privacy commitments:

  • Cloudflare for hosting, content delivery and network protection of the Maha Hub website and applications.
  • Stripe for payment processing. Payment details are entered directly with Stripe, which is responsible for protecting card data.
  • A cloud database and sign-in service for storing customer business data and checking who may open it.
  • An email delivery service for sending sign-in codes and Enewletters.

We review providers before adopting them and keep the list as short as the service allows.

Shared responsibility

Security works best as a partnership. Maha Hub is responsible for the security of the service. Each customer is responsible for how the service is used within its hotel, including:

  • choosing strong passwords and keeping them private;
  • assigning the right role to each user and reviewing access regularly;
  • removing access for staff who leave;
  • keeping the devices used to access Maha secure and up to date;
  • entering only the guest and staff information it is entitled to collect.

Incident response

If we become aware of a security incident affecting customer data, we will investigate promptly, take steps to contain it, and notify affected customers without undue delay with the information we have at the time. Where the law requires us to notify a regulator or affected individuals, we will do so within the required period, or support the customer in doing so where the customer is responsible for that notice.
