How buying Maha works

What you pay for

Access to Maha software as a subscription, plus any related digital hospitality services agreed in your quote, such as onboarding.

Your quote

Prices depend on your property type, size and the products you choose. Your quote confirms the plan, billing period and currency before you pay.

Payment

Payments are processed securely by a third-party payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.

Changes and cancellation

You can cancel at the end of a billing period. See our Refund and Cancellation Policy and Terms of Service.
