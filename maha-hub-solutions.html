Technology

Maha Solutions

Connected software for hotels and accommodation businesses. Every product draws on the same hotel data, so that no team ever enters the same detail twice.

One booking, followed through every department

Information flows from one product to the next.

This is how a single guest booking is designed to travel through Maha Solutions, from first discovery to follow-up. Maha PMS is available today; the other stages arrive as each product is released.

Guest finds youMaha Reach
Guest booksMaha Booking or a channel via Maha Connect
Reservation arrivesMaha PMS
Stay and billingMaha PMS: front office, folio, housekeeping
Follow upMaha Reach and Maha Sales

Who it is for

Solutions for every kind of hotel.

From a single independent property to a group of hotels, the same products grow with you.

Independent hotels

Run the front office, reservations, housekeeping and billing from one system, with direct booking on your own website planned through Maha Booking.

Resorts

Bring rooms, guests and folios together in Maha PMS, with food and beverage and inventory planned to connect to the same data.

Boutique hotels

An uncluttered workspace for small teams, with direct booking and channel management planned to champion your own brand.

Serviced apartments

Manage units, stays, guests and billing in one place, from short visits to extended stays.

Hotel groups

Give each property its own workspace while keeping data organised under one group, with user roles per team.

Multi-property operators

Use the multi-property capability of Maha PMS, with management reporting planned in Maha Manage.
