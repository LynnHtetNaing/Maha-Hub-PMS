Technology

Maha Solutions

Connected software for hotels and accommodation businesses. Every product works from the same hotel data, so teams stop typing the same thing twice.

One booking, followed through the hotel

Data moves from one product to the next.

This is how a single guest booking travels through Maha Solutions.

Guest finds youMaha Reach
Guest booksMaha Booking or a channel via Maha Connect
Reservation arrivesMaha PMS
Stay and billingMaha PMS: front office, folio, housekeeping
Follow upMaha Reach and Maha Sales

Who it is for

Solutions for every kind of hotel.

From one independent property to a group of hotels, the same products grow with you.

Independent hotels

Run the front office, reservations, housekeeping and billing from one system, and take direct bookings on your own website.

Resorts

Bring rooms, guests and folios together in Maha PMS, with food and beverage and inventory planned to connect to the same data.

Boutique hotels

A clear, simple workspace for small teams, with direct booking and channel management to support your own brand.

Serviced apartments

Manage units, stays, guests and billing in one place, from short stays to longer ones.

Hotel groups

Give each property its own workspace while keeping data organised under one group, with user roles per team.

Multi-property operators

Use the multi-property capability of Maha PMS, with management reporting planned in Maha Manage.
