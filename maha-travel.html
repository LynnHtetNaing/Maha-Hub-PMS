Maha Travel

Our own online travel agency, designed to bring guests and Maha hotels together directly. Maha Travel is in development and is not yet available.

Our own channel

A travel channel built with hotels, for hotels.

Maha Travel will give guests a refined way to discover and book Maha hotels, and give hotels a channel they are part of rather than one they simply pay.

It is designed to sit at the front of the Maha platform: reservations arrive directly in Maha PMS, and rates and availability stay aligned through Maha Connect. We will share dates and details here as they are confirmed.
