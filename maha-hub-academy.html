Education

Maha Hub Academy

Practical hospitality education for people starting a career, for hotel teams and for managers. Programmes are opening soon.

Our approach

Learning built around real hotel work.

Hotels are run by people, and well-prepared people make guests feel cared for. The Academy is being built to teach what hotels actually need.

  • Practical and hands-on, not only theory
  • Standards that match how modern hotels operate
  • Training on the tools hotels use, including Maha PMS
  • A clear path from first job to team leader to manager

Programme details, dates and fees will be published here when they are confirmed.

Learning areas

What we plan to teach.

Front office

Reservations, arrivals and departures, billing and guest service at the front desk.

Housekeeping

Room standards, cleaning routines and working with the front desk.

Food and beverage service

Service standards, guest care and the basics of running an outlet.

Hotel management and leadership

Leading a team, controlling cost and growing revenue.

Guest service and communication

Listening, problem solving and care for every guest.

Hotel technology

Using modern hotel systems with confidence, including training on Maha PMS.

Who it is for

For every stage of a hospitality career.

Career starters

School leavers and graduates who want a solid start in hotels.

Hotel teams

Front-line staff who want to improve skills and confidence together.

Managers and owners

Leaders who want better systems, standards and results.
