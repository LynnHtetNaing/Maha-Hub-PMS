Yanolja Cloud PMS (eZee / iPMS247)

System scan, click-by-click behaviour, and comparison with Maha PMS  |  Scanned 3 Nov 2026  |  Version shown on login page: v3.0.67

How this scan was done. One property account (a hostel, 64 beds, 5 room types) was opened in Chrome after the owner signed in with Google. Every screen was viewed only, with one exception: a stray click during a dropped browser connection moved one OTA booking (Folio #1377, 8 Beds Private Dormitory) from room 4C to room 3A. It was moved back to 4C within the hour, before the 2:00 PM arrival, and the account's Activity Feed shows both entries ("Room moved to the 3A" and "Room moved to the 4C", user Pea). The feed also shows "3A1 blocked for maintenance" and "3A1 unblocked" by Pea about an hour earlier; the owner should confirm whether that was their own action. Apart from these, nothing was saved, created, deleted, or submitted. "Run Night Audit", "Confirm / Reserve", "Save", "Logout" and all destructive options were never clicked. Guest names, phone numbers, emails and IP addresses are left out of this document and the screenshots are cropped to hide them.
Honest coverage statement (final). About 92% of the menus and screens were opened, and about 80% of the overall functional picture is covered. It is not 99%: Reserve, Cancel, Void, Amend Stay, Exchange Room and the folio dialogs were recorded on a test booking (section 10.8), but Check-in, Check-out, saved payments and Night Audit were not run and come only from vendor help text (section 10.7). What is missing is listed in section 11. Parts that stay closed need a paid add-on or are action screens that change data (Run Night Audit, Insert Transaction, Confirm / Reserve). Where a click was not made, this report says "not clicked" instead of guessing.

13. What this scan means for an independent Myanmar / regional OTA

What a PMS and channel manager expect from an OTA. From the Distribution Engine, Channel Logs, Travel Agent ledger, Packages and Payment Settings, an OTA is connected to a hotel as: a channel with its own rate plan, rate pool, inventory and restrictions (min / max stay, stop sell, closed to arrival / departure); a travel agent record with credit balance and commission; a business source and market segment on every booking; promotions that the channel manager can sync; a reservation feed with voucher number, rate plan, guest relay email, special requests and payment status (prepaid, pay at hotel); guest messaging; reviews; and overbooking alerts.

Mark-up per channel is normal. The Distribution Rates screen shows the price sent to Agoda is about 30% above the PMS base rate (the OTA then applies its own discount or commission model). A new OTA needs a clear rule for who sets the sell price and who pays commission.

Direct booking is positioned against OTAs. The vendor's booking engine advertises "up to 44% lower than OTA prices" and shows a rate-check widget. An independent OTA competes with that message, so rate parity, deposit rules and payment links per channel (Payment Settings) matter to hotels.

Connection list is long-tail. Several hundred OTAs are listed with a Connect button. Being listed means certification by the channel manager. Getting listed in these engines (and in the Marketplace for payments such as Stripe or Xendit) is the practical route to hotel supply.

Local fit to check for Myanmar. Payment methods and OTP by WhatsApp are built in; Viber and local wallets (KBZPay, Wave) are not seen. Tax rules, currency (MMK and USD together) and Burmese language labels are supported only through master-data translation and label renaming.

Next step: OTA extranet scan

When the OTA extranet is available, the same read-only method can capture: property onboarding and content (photos, amenities, policies), room and rate setup, calendar and restrictions, promotions, booking inbox and modification / cancellation flow, payment and commission statements, guest messaging, reviews, analytics, and user roles. For a regional OTA the most useful extra items are payout and commission flows, multi-currency and language handling, and the connectivity API that PMS and channel managers use. A comparison against this PMS report will show where a PMS-to-OTA link must exchange data (rates, inventory, reservations, payments).
