- Registered office: the Yangon address is shown. The Bangkok Operation Office is shown with its phone number. Confirm both are correct and complete.

## Legal pages
- Terms of Service, governing law and jurisdiction: left out of the page on the owner's decision (no placeholder shows). Add them only with advice from legal counsel.
